Replying to an invitation

  1. Open the link the couple sent (WhatsApp, SMS or email).
  2. Choose Joyfully accept or Regretfully decline, the number of seats, and any dietary needs.
  3. Tap Send RSVP. You can come back to the same link and change your answer.
A general invitation link (from the QR code) is also available. People already on the guest list are asked to use their personal link, so nobody can change someone else's answer.

Sharing photos on the day

  1. Scan the QR code on the TV screen or table card.
  2. Add your name for the credit and choose up to 10 photos (JPG, PNG or WebP, 8 MB each).
  3. Photos appear on the big screen once the couple approves them.
