# 00 · Brief digest

Sources: `Wedding Diary Pitch Final.pdf` (51 pp, "WeddingOS.ai" pitch), `Wedding Diary Bangladesh - App UI (1).pdf` (80 pp, UI/UX case study by Kazi Ahnaf Aquib, March 2026), plus the brand audit and content strategy (23 Sep 2026).

## Client
**Wedding Diary Bangladesh** (weddingdiary.com.bd, @weddingdiarybd), a premium wedding photography and cinematography studio in Banani, Dhaka. It is pitching "Bangladesh's First AI-Powered Wedding Experience Platform", *"Redefining Wedding Experiences Digitally"*, powered by **WeddingOS.ai**. Market: Bangladesh first, then the South Asian diaspora (UK, US, Canada).

## Pains (quoted, pitch p2)
- "Fragmented Tools: disconnected apps for planning, budget, and guests create chaos."
- "Manual Coordination: endless spreadsheets and phone calls instead of automation."
- "No Real-time Engagement: guests are passive observers."
- "Delayed Media Delivery: waiting weeks for photos and videos kills the post-event buzz."
- "Budget Mismanagement: lack of tracking leads to overspending and financial stress."
- "Modern Weddings Need a Digital Brain."

## Numbers
700,000+ weddings/yr in BD · budgets +15% YoY · TAM $12B+, SAM $4.5B, SOM $150M · targets: 50+ partner venues, 10k+ monthly users, 3× viral coefficient, 500+ premium vendors (Phase 2) · claims: 80% coordination time saved, 90% faster vendor comms, 70% less stress.

## Ecosystem (pitch p5)
Planning · Vendor Marketplace · Live Experience · AI Media Engine · Smart Streaming.

## Phasing in the brief (pitch p23, p48–50)
- **Phase 01 MVP**: Core Planning Tools, Basic Marketplace, initial app. Month 1–4: Dashboard & Booking System, Budget Planner, Guest Management, Itinerary Builder, Vendor Onboarding Portal, Service Listing, Review & Rating, UI/UX finalisation.
- Phase 02 Vendor Scaling (Vendor Dashboard & Analytics, commission booking engine) / AI & Automation (photo engine, gallery, highlights, reels, face recognition).
- Phase 03 Advanced (AR, virtual attendance, translation, analytics, emergency, post-wedding).
- Phase 04 International.

## Brand (App UI deck p3, p27 is the app's design system)
- Coral Red **#D97566** (primary, "prosperity and love in Bengali culture"), Pure White #FFFFFF, Light Coral **#FF8B7A**. Dark navy surfaces on some slides (Card Designer, Catering, Cloud Backup).
- Font: **ITC Avant Garde Gothic** (Light / Regular / Bold), a licensed font.
- 12px rounded corners, glassmorphism cards with soft shadows and blur, smooth micro-interactions, generous whitespace, coral left-bar section titles with italic subtitles.
- Logo: red open-book "WD" monogram (PNG only, logo red #E91C24).
- Voice: warm and story-led ("we"), per the brand audit.

## Inconsistencies (noted politely)
1. The pitch uses **USD** ($45k budget, $2,500 vendors) and bright red #E91C24; the App UI deck uses **BDT** and coral #D97566. We follow the App UI deck for the app.
2. Stats differ between decks (guests 250/450/350, budget ৳6L vs ৳25L vs $45k), so they are treated as illustrative.
3. Pitch pages use Western names (Sarah, Michael); the UI deck uses Bangladeshi names. Seed data uses Bangladeshi names.
4. Two UI deck pages (20 Gift Tracker, 29 Photographer Directory) contain leftover AI-editing prompt text instead of the design.
5. Pitch p8 calls the product "WeddingOS.ai booking engine". Branding is "Wedding Diary, Powered by WeddingOS.ai".
6. Profile setup shows the date "15 December 2024", which is in the past.
